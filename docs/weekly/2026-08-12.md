*Covers roughly 6 - 12 August 2026.*

semester also started.

## 1. Penny

GW and I finished a first draft of the database schema for Penny. The open question
is whether tuition centres should be a second target audience alongside individual
tutors, since that changes how accounts, payments and schedules relate to each other.

## 2. The portfolio backend is done, the hard way

Most of the week went into the portfolio site's backend, and as I expected, it was
not as easy as it looked. It was finally finished on the last day of the week, and it
took far too long because of how oddly the software was structured.

The lesson is simple: **never let AI fully design your application architecture.** A
lot of that structure came from accepting suggestions without thinking hard enough
about how the pieces fit together. AI tools are useful for filling in parts. They are
not the ones who have to live with the design.

## 3. Deploying the backend

The other big blocker was deployment. It follows the same general pattern as the
built-from-source guide (app repo, CI, config repo, ArgoCD), but the guide does not
cover two things this backend needed: **secrets** and **IngressRoutes**. The
deployment guide should be updated to include both.
